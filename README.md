Hi! I'm a Ph.D. student in Data Science at the University of Virginia advised by [Tom Hartvigsen](https://www.tomhartvigsen.com). I work on Benchmarking and Editing/Repairing Multimodal-Language Models, particularly 

- How to improve the external validity of Language Model Benchmarks ([NeurIPS'26](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/paris/poster/139753))
- Making VLMs more editable without affecting their visual grounding (In Progress)

Previously, I've been a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at Columbia University (Department of Economics) for professors [Michael Best](https://econ.columbia.edu/econpeople/michael-best/) (Columbia University), [Jonas Hjort](https://sites.google.com/site/jonashjort/) (University College London) and [Noémie Pinardon-Touati](https://npinardontouati.github.io/), exploring the applications of natural language processing to development economics and macroeconomics.

I've also worked for the Development Impact Evaluation Unit at the World Bank for professor/Lead PI [Daniel L. Chen](https://users.nber.org/~dlchen/) and as research assistant for professors [Kristian Lopez-Vargas](https://kmlv.github.io/) (UCSC) and [Louis Putterman](https://www.brown.edu/academics/population-studies/people/person/louis-putterman) (Brown University).
